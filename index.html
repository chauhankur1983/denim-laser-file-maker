Simulated result on dark indigo

How to get a good file: photograph the reference garment flat, in even daylight, front on. Crop to one panel. Lower the black point until the unfaded indigo turns white in the laser file (no laser), and set the white point at the strongest fade. Use soften for natural whiskers and sharpen for crisp scraping lines. Always run a test on the real fabric: power, speed and fabric change the result.
